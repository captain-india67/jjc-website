The story so far

A hundred years of quietly shaping leaders.

Since 1926, thousands of learners from schools across Johannesburg have come through the council, picking up experience in leadership, public speaking, teamwork, community service and citizenship.

Unlike leadership structures that sit inside a single school, the council brings together learners from different schools and backgrounds. That mix is the point: it builds collaboration, mutual understanding and friendships that last well beyond the council year.

One of the defining chapters came during South Africa's transition to democracy. Before the first democratic elections in 1994, the council asked participating schools to nominate learners with the qualities of a Junior Councillor irrespective of race, despite the legal restrictions of the time, and planned its activities, outings and events so that every councillor could take part together.

Former councillors have gone on to lead in business, law, medicine, education, government, entrepreneurship and community organisations. Many trace part of that to the opportunities, friendships and values they found here.
