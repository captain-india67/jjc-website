What the council is

A seat at the city's table since 1926.

The Johannesburg Junior Council is a youth leadership body made up of 82 Grade 11 learners, nominated by 41 secondary schools in the Greater Johannesburg area. It has been developing the leadership potential of the city's school youth since 1926.

Councillors from different schools and backgrounds work together for about three months, then elect their own leadership: two Co-Mayors, a Chairman of Management, a Chief Whip and the chairpersons of the five committees. A Governing Body of parents and interested members of the public supports the council, which is a registered non-profit.

The work is practical: community service for the under-privileged and senior citizens, leadership lectures, and projects that build councillors' confidence and awareness of the city they live in.

The record

A century, in moments.

One hundred years of quietly shaping young leaders, and a centenary celebrated this September.

1926

Founded in Johannesburg

The Junior Council is established to develop the leadership potential of the city's school youth, bringing together learners from schools across Johannesburg.

Before 1994

Open to every learner

As the country moved towards democracy, the council asked its schools to nominate learners with the qualities of a Junior Councillor irrespective of race, despite the legal restrictions of the time, and planned every activity so that all councillors could take part together.

Today

Five committees, one city

82 councillors from 41 schools, working through the Well-Being, Arts & Culture, Sports & Recreation, Environmental and Outreach committees.

2026

Centenary year

The centenary dinner on Tuesday 22 September at Holy Family College, with keynote speaker Paula Slier, and the 100th birthday Spring Day on Saturday 26 September.
